# ICLR 2027 Final Red-Team Report

Date: 2026-08-31
Paper: Why Weak Features Hide: The Detector Axis Hole Is Not a Free-Decoder Constraint

## Verdict

PASS for submission after targeted claim-boundary corrections, E4-v2 matched-control integration, and the exact plus relaxed spectral-anchor extensions. The completed E4-v2 result is reported. A later independent held-out TopK confirmation completed but failed its pre-specified all-pass gate at d32-k4; Table 6 reports that failure. A transparent post-hoc same-trajectory budget trace reaches dense recovery 0.875 at 16k steps and is reported in Table 7 as a budget diagnostic, without revising the failed original gate. The exact v3 target-free spectral-anchor family and relaxed v4 bounded-drift family each have proved structured-family guarantees and 48/48 supporting rows; v4 permits a trainable anchor and nonzero residual leakage but still does not expand the claim to unconstrained SAE training. The submission artifact is PDF-only; source backups and audit material are kept outside the upload package.

## Corrections Applied

- Clarified that the `lambda^-2` result is a conditional isolation-stage benchmark after a separating direction is available, not an end-to-end trained-SAE guarantee.
- Corrected the Bernoulli-mixture difference-of-means factor to `kappa = lambda sqrt((1-p) N)`; synchronized the first-principles figure overlay and protocol formula.
- Defined the whitened feature strength `tilde lambda_i` rather than reusing the unwhitened strength in the general-background corollary.
- Recast the unconstrained-decoder theorem as a representation/model-class statement, without treating `F_k` as a probability-model projection.
- Restricted the oracle-column alignment claim to absorbed terms with a nonzero component orthogonal to `w`; collinear absorption only changes scale.
- Corrected the proof wording: under Bernoulli shifts, `w^T h` is independent of `P h` with mean zero and variance `1+beta`, but is not itself Gaussian.
- Added a compact provenance note to Table 5: four representative rows are shown and complete nine-atom scores are in the JSON artifact.
- Replaced only the hard-data-error figure assets: the public-Bloom atom diagnostic and the residual-floor/capacity figure, using supplied PNGs copied into stable `*_supplied` assets. Figures with no hard data error were left unchanged.
- Added the completed pre-specified E4-v2 detector--TopK control: independence power 0.081 vs. 1.000 at matched strength, both geometries meeting the frozen cosine criterion in all eight seeds, and 19/24 recovery in each of the two three-strength detector classes.
- Added and executed a separate held-out confirmation protocol with a contract-tested runner. Its 64-row artifact is complete, but its all-pass gate is false because d32-k4 dense recovery is 0.625 below the frozen 0.75 minimum. A separate 48-row post-hoc d32-k4 checkpoint trace reproduces every 4k trajectory and reaches dense recovery 0.875 at 16k; Table 7 reports it as a budget diagnostic, not as a revised confirmation decision. The corrected v3 objective/capacity stress matrix is reported in Table 8.
- Renamed the paper to distinguish detector-class axis holes from representation constraints on free sparse decoders.
- Added an objective-agnostic reconstruction certificate theorem: for any frozen decoder subspace, independent held-out excess risk gives a target-alignment lower bound under an isotropic rank-one covariance model. Added a complementary structural-constraint proposition showing that the unconstrained feasible decoder class alone gives no positive uniform alignment bound; this does not rule out data-dependent learners. Added a relaxed bounded-drift spectral-anchor theorem and v4 experiment: the anchor is trainable inside a training-only trust region and residual leakage is bounded rather than zero. This is a post-training certificate, not a claim that every objective converges. A frozen 48-run objective/capacity stress matrix tests the transfer: the corrected v3 certificate pass rate is 19/48, so the result exposes objective/capacity dependence rather than supporting universal training recovery; the synthetic demo is explicitly evidence-ineligible. The earlier v2 summary used a pre-correction projector-difference bound and is superseded.

## Claim Boundary

The paper does not claim a new unconstrained SAE optimizer, a bake-off, a trained-SAE lambda^-4 theorem, residual equals dark matter, named-concept discovery, or recovery equivalence for all unconstrained SAE objectives. The objective-agnostic certificate is conditional on a valid independent risk estimate, covariance model, rank, and target-specific oracle comparison; it does not establish optimization convergence. The spectral-anchor theorem is universal only over the explicitly defined v3 anchor-preserving family: training-only spectral anchor, signed anchor slot, orthogonal residual decoder, finite-sample eigengap, and family-wise confidence. The v4 extension is universal only over the bounded-drift family: training-only reference anchor, trainable trust-region radius, bounded residual leakage, finite-sample eigengap, and family-wise confidence. E4-v2 is a pre-specified matched TopK control; GPT-2/Pythia and public-atom results remain specified-direction/known-direction diagnostics. The held-out capacity/sparsity confirmation completed but did not pass all gates. The post-hoc d32-k4 trace supports a budget explanation only and does not broaden the unconstrained-SAE claim or revise that gate.
